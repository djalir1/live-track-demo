In one page

What Live Track does

A GPS tracker in each bus reports where the bus is every few seconds. On every trip, the bus attendant marks each child on and off. Live Track puts the two together: the school and the parents always know where the bus is and who is on it.

Where is the bus?

  • Live position, speed and engine on/off from the tracker.
  • Knows when the bus is at each stop on its route.
  • Works out the distance and minutes to each parent's stop.

Who is on it?

  • The attendant taps each child On and Off.
  • The parent gets a message within seconds.
  • The school sees on board / still to pick up / dropped off.

The key rule: attendance is counted per trip, not per day. Morning (home → school) and afternoon (school → home) are two separate trips. A child driven to school by car in the morning and bussed home in the afternoon is never marked absent.
Accounts are created by the school, there is no public sign-up. Parents and attendants receive a 6-digit activation code and choose their own password the first time. The same code is used to reset a forgotten password. A code is valid for 30 days and locks after 5 wrong tries.

The people

Four sides, one journey

Each person sees only what they need. A parent only ever sees their own children and their bus.

School office (admin)

  • Registers buses, routes, stops, drivers, attendants, students and parents.
  • Sees every bus live with on board / expected / absent counts.
  • Handles safety alerts: wrong bus, no-show, child left on the bus.
  • Sends messages to the parents of one bus or of all buses.
  • Downloads attendance reports in Excel and PDF.

Parent

  • Follows the bus live and sees km and minutes to their stop.
  • Is told when the child boards, arrives at school and gets off.
  • Says "not riding today" for one trip or a whole day.
  • Receives delay and incident messages from the bus.
  • One account for all their children.

Bus attendant

  • Sees the trip list: which children to pick up at each stop.
  • Taps each child On and Off.
  • Sends one-tap announcements: traffic, late, accident, breakdown.
  • Does the final "bus is empty" check to close the trip.

Driver and student

  • Driver: just drives. The tracker wired into the bus reports the position by itself.
  • Student: needs no phone. The attendant marks them.
  • Later, a fingerprint reader at the door will do the attendant's tap (under construction).
Safety rule: the driver drives, the attendant marks. If the check-in screen is opened by the driver, it locks while the bus is moving.

For the school office · once

Setting up your school

Sign in at /auth and work down the left menu: first Fleet, then Students & parents. Press Ctrl K at any time to search for a page, student, parent or bus.

Add the buses Fleet → Vehicles

Name, plate and number of seats. Enter the tracker's 15-digit IMEI (on its label), then send the tracker the setup SMS shown on the page. The bus switches from Waiting for the tracker to Connected.

Draw the routes and stops Fleet → Routes & stops

One route per bus. Add the stops in morning order on the map (first pick-up → … → school) and finish with the school stop. Each stop has a circle: when the bus enters it, Live Track knows it is at that stop.

Add drivers and attendants Fleet → Drivers / Bus attendants

Assign each to a bus. For an attendant, enter their phone number: a 6-digit activation code appears. Give it to them.

Register the students Students & parents → Students

Add student, or Import CSV for a whole list. For each child:

  • name, class, student number, and a photo only with the parent's consent;
  • bus, morning pick-up stop and afternoon drop-off stop (they can differ);
  • one or two parents with their phone numbers: brothers and sisters share the same parent;
  • a note for the attendant, e.g. "allergic to peanuts", "gets off with her sister".

Invite the parents Students & parents → Parents

Each new parent gets an activation code. Copy the ready-made WhatsApp / SMS message and send it. The page shows who has activated the app; Reset password gives a parent a new code.

Ready. From the next morning, attendants open their trip list and parents start receiving messages.

For parents and attendants

Getting into the app

No sign-up and no email needed. Your school gives you a code, you choose your password once.

1 · Receive a code

The school sends you a message: "Your Live Track code is 482 913…"

2 · First time here?

Parents open /parent, attendants /auth. Tap First time here? (attendants: Activate your account).

3 · Choose a password

Enter your phone number, the 6-digit code and a new password. You are signed in.

Next times

Phone number + password. The phone number can be typed any way: 0788 200 001, +250788200001 or 788200001.

Forgot the password?

Ask the school office. They press Reset password on your name and give you a new code; you then follow the same 3 steps.

Install it like an app

PhoneHow
AndroidOpen the site in Chrome, tap Install app at the bottom (or menu ⋮ → Install app).
iPhoneOpen the site in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Then allow notifications when asked (or later in ⚙ Settings), so messages arrive even when the app is closed.

Codes are personal. A code works for 30 days, only for the phone number it was made for, and locks after 5 wrong tries. Never share it.

Start the trip

Sign in, tap Start morning trip (or the Afternoon tab). Children are grouped by stop, in route order. Parents are told the bus has started.

At each stop

The stop the bus is at jumps to the top by itself. Tap On when a child gets on, Not here if they don't come. Children whose parent said not riding are already greyed out.

Something happens on the road

Announce → Heavy traffic +10 / +20 / +30 min · Running late · Accident (all safe) · Breakdown · your own text. Every parent on the bus is told; accident and breakdown also alert the school.

At school (morning)

All off at school marks every child on board as dropped off. Afternoon: tap Off here for each child at their stop.

End the trip

Tap End trip, walk to the back of the bus and confirm "the bus is empty". A trip cannot close while a child is still marked on board.

Made a mistake? The ↶ button undoes the last tap; the parent receives a short correction. A child who is not on your list (wrong bus) can still be added: search the name, the school is told.

Help

Questions and quick fixes

The answers to what people ask most in the first weeks.

I can't sign in

Check the phone number and that you are on the right page: parents at /parent, staff at /auth. Still stuck: ask the school for a new code.

I get no notifications

Open ⚙ Settings and turn notifications on, and allow them in the phone settings. On iPhone, the app must be added to the Home Screen first.

The bus shows "Connection lost"

The tracker has no mobile data for a while (tunnel, weak signal, SIM credit). It reconnects by itself; the last known position is shown meanwhile.

My child is not on the list

The attendant can search and add any child of the school; the school is told so the records can be fixed.

The minutes to my stop jump

They are worked out from the live position and road speed. Traffic changes them; they settle as the bus gets close.

The trip won't close

A child is still marked on board. Mark them off (or check the bus!), then confirm "the bus is empty".

A parent changed phone number

Students & parents → Parents: edit the number, then Reset password to send a new code.

Fingerprint check-in?

Under construction. Consent is already recorded at registration; when the readers are installed they replace the attendant's tap, everything else stays the same.

Privacy. Each parent sees only their own children and their bus. Photos are stored only with the parent's consent. The school can see and correct every record.
